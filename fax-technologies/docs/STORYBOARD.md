# Storyboard

This document defines the narrative flow and motion blueprint for the experience.

## Narrative Arc
1. Loading
2. Hero
3. Idea
4. Evolution
5. AI Brain
6. Solutions
7. Portfolio
8. Dashboard
9. Contact
10. End

## Scene Notes

### 1. Loading
- Purpose: establish tone and brand atmosphere.
- Mood: cinematic, minimal, immersive.
- Motion: slow fade and subtle ambient drift.

### 2. Hero
- Purpose: present the editorial headline and brand promise.
- Mood: confident and premium.
- Motion: reveal text, proof strip, and atmospheric background.

### 3. Idea
- Purpose: communicate the core concept with clarity.
- Mood: thoughtful and intelligent.
- Motion: layered text and card transitions.

### 4. Evolution
- Purpose: show progression and development.
- Mood: dynamic and purposeful.
- Motion: scroll-linked timeline or milestone reveals.

### 5. AI Brain
- Purpose: make intelligence feel tangible and modern.
- Mood: futuristic and precise.
- Motion: abstract, responsive visuals and light pulses.

### 6. Solutions
- Purpose: explain the offering in a structured, digestible way.
- Mood: clean and reassuring.
- Motion: staggered card reveals and soft parallax.

### 7. Portfolio
- Purpose: demonstrate proof through featured work.
- Mood: polished and high-end.
- Motion: cinematic transitions and focus shifts.

### 8. Dashboard
- Purpose: show the product experience in action.
- Mood: clear and functional.
- Motion: layered interface reveal and subtle motion.

### 9. Contact
- Purpose: end with a clear conversion point.
- Mood: direct and welcoming.
- Motion: calm final motion with strong CTA emphasis.

## Scroll Guidance
- Every section should feel intentional.
- Movement should support the story rather than distract from it.
- Use transition pacing to guide the user naturally between scenes.
