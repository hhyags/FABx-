# Storyboard & Motion Bible

- Camera position: centered, slow drift
- Camera movement: subtle easing in
- Scroll percentage: 0-8%
- Objects: ambient light, abstract shapes, brand mark
- Lighting: low contrast, cinematic glow
- Animation timing: 1.2s fade-in
- Text content: brand introduction
- Interaction: none
- Transition: move into hero reveal
